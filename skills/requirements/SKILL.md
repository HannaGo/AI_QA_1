# Requirements Grooming

Analyze, clarify, and refine raw product requirements into structured acceptance criteria. Identify missing details and ambiguity early in the process.

Output a groomed requirements document containing:
- Clear, unambiguous user stories or requirements
- Acceptance criteria for each requirement
- Open questions or risks flagged for clarification
